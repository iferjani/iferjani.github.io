About

I am Dr. Imen Ferjani (she/her), a researcher in mathematics at the Laboratory of Physics-Mathematics of the Faculty of Sciences of Sfax (FSS), University of Sfax, Tunisia.

My work lies in functional analysis and operator theory. I study spectral properties of bounded and unbounded operators on Banach spaces: essential spectra, Fredholm and Browder theory, pseudospectra and condition pseudospectra, measures of noncompactness (Kuratowski, Hausdorff, De Blasi) and the various classes of weakly, demi- and order-demicompact operators that they generate. A large part of my research concerns block operator matrices, linear relations, lattice-normed spaces and Banach lattices, and the stability of spectra under perturbation and coperturbation. More recently I have been adding numerical simulations to this program, from many operators to operator-theoretic models of stability in neural networks.

I serve as an editor of the Advanced Studies: Euro-Tbilisi Mathematical Journal (Tbilisi Centre for Mathematical Sciences) since 2024, and I have reviewed for the Journal of Inequalities and Applications, Abstract and Applied Analysis and ASETMJ.
